## Step into Your Future

Whether you’re applying to college for the first time, continuing your education, or returning to school for an advanced degree, you deserve a relevant, high-quality education that will prepare you for any direction you want to take your life and career. The University of Arizona Global Campus allows you to take one class at a time as a full-time student, with a convenient online classroom accessible anywhere, at any time, so you can balance work, life, and school.

Learn More

## Reasons to Choose UAGC

No SAT, GMAT, GRE, or ACT Required

Standardized test scores are not required for enrollment.

5- to 6-week Classes

Take one class at a time as a full-time student.

$0 Application Fee

Apply to UAGC risk-free.

* **Accreditation** - UAGC is WSCUC-accredited.
* **Generous Transfer Policy** - Transfer approved college credits toward your bachelor’s program at UAGC.\*
* **Credit for Work/Life Experience** - Earn college credit for relevant work/life experience through the Prior Learning Assessment.
* **Convenient and Flexible** - Balance work, life, family, and college while pursuing your online degree.
* **Our Promise** - Attend for 3 weeks risk-free to see if UAGC is right for you.
* **Innovative Technology** - The UAGC platform lets you study anywhere you go and access the online classroom 24/7.
* **Learn from the Best** - Master your field with instruction from seasoned faculty with experience practicing in their respective specialties.

## The Benefits of a Global Campus Degree

With more than 50 online degree programs to choose from, Global Campus delivers relevant, up-to-date information you can apply in the real world. From opening up new opportunities with an online bachelor’s degree to setting yourself apart from the competition with an online master’s degree or achieving the pinnacle of education and expertise with an online doctoral degree, your future awaits at Global Campus. Global Campus is accredited by WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC). WSCUC is an institutional accrediting body recognized by the U.S. Department of Education (ED) and the Council on Higher Education Accreditation (CHEA).  Additionally, many online degrees offered at UAGC received programmatic accreditation.
