Executive Summary

Contentsquare UX Intelligence — 30-Day Window

  • RFI forms are the #1 rage-click zone site-wide. The two-step RFI form (form-twostep__form) on Paid Success pages logged 118,138 clicks with a 3.23× recurrence rate — users are repeatedly clicking/tapping the same form elements, signaling confusion or broken interactions.
  • Quote slider is the most-clicked frustration element globally. With 67,792 clicks at 3.55× recurrence, the quote-slider module triggers repeated taps likely from non-responsive carousel controls.
  • RFI Pop-Up form has a 2.78× click recurrence across 54,090 clicks — the form's submit or field interactions are confusing users at massive scale.
  • Application Pages have 71.7% bounce rate — the highest of any page group — with only 32.3% scroll depth, suggesting the Salesforce intake flow loses users immediately.
  • Paid Success pages load in 6.70s (nearly 2× the blog's 4.12s), contributing to the 11.7% bounce rate and reducing the value of paid traffic investments.

Load Time vs Bounce Correlation

Pages loading under 4.2s (Blog, Application) average 44.5% bounce while pages above 6.5s (Success, Homepage, Paid, RFI Pop-Up) average 18.5% bounce. The inverse correlation is because high-bounce Application Pages are Salesforce-hosted (fast but immediately abandoned), while slow-but-sticky pages like Paid Success hold users with form interactions despite load times. Load time is a friction factor on marketing pages but not the primary bounce driver — content relevance and form UX matter more.

Critical: Paid Landing Page Form Friction

The two-step form on paid success pages is the most interacted-with element on the entire site (118,138 clicks, 89.2% exposure, 53.8% click rate) but converts at only 0.04% per click with a 3.23× recurrence rate. Users click the form 3+ times on average before abandoning. This is the #1 opportunity on the site: every 1% improvement in form conversion from these clicks would translate to ~1,181 additional leads per month. Investigate: field validation errors, dropdown usability, mobile tap targets, and step-2 abandonment.

Key Findings

1
Form Rage Clicks Are the #1 Issue Site-Wide

Three form zones — form-twostep__form (3.23×), webform-submission RFI pop-up (2.78×), and paid-twostep__form (2.41×) — collectively generate 181,000+ frustrated clicks per month. All share click recurrence above 2.4× with conversion rates under 0.3%. The form experience is the single biggest conversion bottleneck.

2
Application Pages: 71.7% Bounce, 485K Views

The Salesforce-hosted application flow has the highest bounce rate of any page group, with nearly half a million monthly views bleeding out. Only 32.3% scroll depth suggests users either can't find what they need or immediately disengage from the application form. This is a massive funnel leak between RFI completion and application start.

3
Quote Slider Generates False Engagement

The testimonial/quote slider is the most-clicked non-form element (67,792 clicks, 3.55× recurrence) but has only 0.11% conversion after click. Users tap repeatedly trying to advance slides — likely because carousel controls aren't responsive or obvious. This creates noise in engagement metrics without driving conversions.

4
Blog Sidebar Form: High Engagement, Zero Conversion

The blog draws 44,598 views with only 17.3% bounce (best engagement tier), but the sidebar RFI form has 2.58× rage clicks and literally 0.00% conversion after click. Blog readers are primed but the form mechanism is broken or misaligned with their intent.

5
Mobile Parity Risk

With 47.9% of traffic on mobile (509,924 views), every form usability issue is amplified. The 2.41× recurrence on the sticky RFI header form on degree pages and the pop-up form friction are likely worse on smaller viewports where tap targets are compressed. Mobile-specific zoning data would quantify the gap.
