Executive Summary

Five signals that define the UX friction landscape across 1.07M monthly pageviews.

  1. RFI forms are the #1 rage-click zone site-wide. The two-step form on Paid Success pages logged 118,138 clicks with a 3.23× recurrence rate — users repeatedly clicking the same elements, signaling confusion or broken interactions.
  2. Quote slider is the most-clicked frustration element globally. 67,792 clicks at 3.55× recurrence — repeated taps from non-responsive carousel controls.
  3. RFI Pop-Up form has 2.78× click recurrence across 54,090 clicks — submit or field interactions confusing users at massive scale.
  4. Application Pages: 71.7% bounce rate — highest of any page group — with only 32.3% scroll depth. The Salesforce intake flow loses users immediately.
  5. Paid Success pages load in 6.70s (nearly 2× the blog's 4.12s), reducing the value of paid traffic investments.

Load Time vs Bounce

Pages loading under 4.2s average 44.5% bounce while pages above 6.5s average 18.5% bounce. The inverse correlation exists because high-bounce Application Pages are Salesforce-hosted (fast but immediately abandoned), while slow pages like Paid Success hold users with form interactions. Content relevance and form UX matter more than load time alone.

Key Findings

1
Form Rage Clicks Are the #1 Issue Site-Wide

Three form zones — form-twostep__form (3.23×), webform-submission RFI pop-up (2.78×), and paid-twostep__form (2.41×) — collectively generate 181,000+ frustrated clicks per month with conversion rates under 0.3%.

2
Application Pages: 71.7% Bounce, 485K Views

The Salesforce-hosted application flow has the highest bounce rate of any page group. Only 32.3% scroll depth suggests users can't find what they need or immediately disengage. Massive funnel leak between RFI completion and application start.

3
Quote Slider Generates False Engagement

67,792 clicks at 3.55× recurrence but only 0.11% conversion. Users tap repeatedly trying to advance slides — carousel controls aren't responsive or obvious. Creates noise in engagement metrics.

4
Blog Sidebar Form: High Engagement, Zero Conversion

44,598 views with 17.3% bounce (best engagement) but the sidebar form has 2.58× rage clicks and literally 0.00% conversion. Engaged readers, broken capture mechanism.

5
Mobile Parity Risk

47.9% of traffic on mobile (509,924 views). Every form issue is amplified on smaller viewports where tap targets are compressed. Mobile-specific zoning data would quantify the gap.
