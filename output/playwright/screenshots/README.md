### Key Paid Insights

- **degree-programs-v7 is the model**: 13.57% conv with a program category browser that lets visitors self-select. 4.5x the generic RFI page.
- **college-courses-v5 proves content-rich pages justify higher CPCs**: $1.64M spend but 7.25% conv and 74.5% engagement.
- **request-info-v5 has the most volume but worst rate**: 1.18M sessions mostly from Paid Social (cold traffic), 53% bounce. The 3.01% rate is partly depressed by $243K in awareness spend (YT/CTV/Audio) with 0 conversions.
- **bachelors-degrees-v5 has the worst engagement among high-spend pages**: $1.41M spend, 57% bounce, $514/conversion.
- The **4.5x gap** between degree-programs-v7 (13.6%) and request-info-v5 (3.0%) is largely channel mix (Display vs Paid Social), not purely page quality.
