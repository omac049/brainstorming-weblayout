Key Takeaways for UAGC
Form Weight
UAGC's 2-step form is mid-pack. Empire State: 4 fields. SNHU: 14. CSU Global and UT Tyler: 5–6 required. No competitor uses a two-step wizard like UAGC — WGU's 3-step is the exception, on a separate subdomain.
Form-in-Hero
Only UT Tyler embeds the RFI directly in the hero (Slate). SNHU and WGU link out. CSU Global just below fold. Empire State sidebar. UAGC's on-page approach is not unusual but the 3.23x rage-click rate suggests execution problems, not strategy problems.
Mobile CTA
No competitor has a sticky bottom CTA bar consuming 45% of mobile viewport. CSU Global persists header CTAs; UT Tyler desktop-only. This is a UAGC-specific friction pattern.
Chat
4 of 5 competitors have visible chatbots. UAGC has none on audited pages — a notable gap.
Personalization
None show dynamic first-visit personalization. Empire State's "What Brings You Here?" tiles and UT Tyler's form triage are the strongest signals. SNHU carries browsing context into the RFI form. UAGC lacks audience triage or context carry-through.
