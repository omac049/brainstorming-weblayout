Phase 1 Outcome

  • Complete visual record of every page at desktop and mobile viewports, each annotated with Contentsquare scroll depth, activity rate, and load time
  • 26-module component taxonomy with per-page tagging and cross-reference matrix
  • RFI content audit with CS form frustration overlay — pages rated "Good" for content still show 2.41× form rage-clicks, proving form UX is a bottleneck independent of surrounding content
  • All 10 paid landing pages compared across GA4 + Contentsquare metrics (3 sortable CS columns); tier differences reflect content quality around a universally struggling form
  • Clear recommendation: enrich thin pages with PROG-01 category grid and PROG-02 program list from top performers
  • Contentsquare zoning analysis: form rage-clicks (3.23× recurrence, 118K clicks) are the #1 friction signal — two-step RFI form on paid pages converts at 0.04% per click; blog sidebar form at 2.58× with 0% conversion
  • Cross-cutting finding: 48% mobile traffic shares all form friction — every 1% improvement in paid form conversion = ~1,181 additional leads/month
