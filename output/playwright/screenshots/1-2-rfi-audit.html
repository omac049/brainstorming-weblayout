Executive summary

  • Pages that expose program discovery content before pushing the RFI consistently convert roughly 3–5× better than thin, form-first layouts with the same template family.
  • The top paid landing by volume (request-info-v5, ~1.18M sessions) fails the 5-second test: generic headline, no program grid, accreditation and proof far from the form.
  • Best paid pattern: degree-programs-v7 front-loads the program category grid and deep program list so visitors self-select before the ask — #1 paid conversion rate in the set.
  • Organic /online-degrees (~88.8% engaged sessions, strong conv) validates a content-first hub pattern at essentially zero media cost.
  • Blog templates (~243K sessions across high-traffic posts in this audit) pair strong readership with near-zero conversion: generic sidebar RFI and disconnected in-article CTAs.
