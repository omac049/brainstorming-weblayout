The Data Says Fix the Funnel
~1 min
💡

You're picking up from: "Omar has been leading the work on analyzing the current layout… He's going to walk you through what we're seeing at a more detailed level… I'll hand it over to you." — Smile, take a beat, then go.

🗣

"Thanks [Name]. So — I've been living inside this data for a while now. GSC, GA4, Contentsquare, Google Ads… basically every tool that can tell us what people actually do when they land on our site."

🗣

"And the short version is: we're really good at getting people to the front door. Eleven million sessions, almost nine million in ad spend — people show up. The problem is what happens next."

💥

"So I'm gonna walk you through what we found, where we're leaving the most on the table, and then a concrete plan to fix it. I'll try to keep it tight — but if something catches your attention, jump in."

💡

Under 60 seconds. You're setting the room up, not making the case yet. Conversational, relaxed. The "jump in" line gives ELT permission to engage — they'll respect you for it.

➡

"Let me start with the single biggest thing we found."

2
4.5x Conversion Gap
~1.5 min
🎯

Let the "4.5x" sit on screen for a beat. Don't rush to explain it. Let the room read it.

🗣

"So our main landing page — the one that gets over a million sessions a year — converts at 3%. Meanwhile, another page on the exact same site, with similar form elements, converts at 13.6%. That's a 4.5x gap. Between our own pages."

💥

"Same school. Similar forms. The difference is what the visitor sees before they ever get to a form. One page earns attention. The other one just… asks."

Blog tease (the insight block)
🗣

"And it's not just paid pages. We've got a PhD blog post — ranks number three on Google, 149,000 people read it last year, they stay for eight minutes. And it generated 21 leads. There's no CTA on it. Not a bad CTA — no CTA. We just never asked."

"Honestly? No. And that's not the target. If we get the main page from 3% to even 7 — half the best page — that doubles our conversions. I'll show you the math on that later."

➡

"Let me zoom out and give you a sense of the full picture."

3
The Scale of the Opportunity
~2 min
Top-line numbers (left side of slide)
🗣

"So here's what we're working with. 11 million sessions, 1.7 million organic clicks, 348,000 conversions. This is not a small site. People are showing up."

🎯

Point to the engagement rate — tap it if you're near the screen.

💥

"But engagement is 47%. That means more than half the people who visit don't meaningfully interact. And 60% of our traffic is mobile, where engagement drops another 6 points. So… most of our visitors are on the worst-performing device for our site. That's a lot of wasted arrivals."

Channel insight (right side of slide)
🗣

"Quick channel view — Paid Search and Display are doing their jobs. Good engagement, solid conversions."

💥

"But Paid Social — which is our largest paid channel at almost 2 million sessions — only gets 42% engagement. 58% bounce. We're paying to drive people to pages that aren't built for them, and more than half just leave."

"Yeah — organic is actually declining pretty sharply. Homepage is down 42% half-over-half, blog down almost 50%. That's likely AI overview cannibalization — Google answering questions before anyone clicks. I have a slide on that coming up, but the short version is: fewer free visits makes conversion efficiency even more urgent."

➡

"Let me show you what I mean. Here are two of our pages side by side."

4
Tale of Two Pages
~2 min
🎯

Point to the LEFT screenshot. Let the room see it.

🗣

"Okay, this is where it gets real. This is our main landing page — request-info-v5. Over a million sessions, mostly from Paid Social. 53% bounce, 3% conversion. And look at it. You land, you see a form, some generic copy. That's it. No program info, no salary data, no 'here's why UAGC.' Just… 'give us your info.'"

🎯

Now point RIGHT.

🗣

"Now this one — degree-programs-v7 — 222,000 sessions, 25% bounce, 13.6% conversion. The difference? You land and there's a program browser. You can explore degrees, see what you'd earn, read outcomes. By the time you reach the form, you've already sold yourself."

💥

"The forms are similar. The layout is completely different. One page earns your attention before it asks for anything. The other one opens with 'hi, can I have your phone number?' — and 53% of people say no."

💡

The "hi, can I have your phone number" line usually gets a laugh. If it lands, ride it — it makes the point memorable.

"Traffic source is a factor, sure. But it doesn't explain a 4.5x gap. Even Paid Search — highest-intent visitors — only converts at 3.5% on the bachelor's page. Content-rich pages outperform regardless of source. The page is the variable."

➡

"Now you might be thinking, 'okay, that's two pages.' Fair. Let me show you it's not an anomaly."

5
It's a System-Wide Gap
~1.5 min
🎯

Walk through the table top to bottom. Let the green highlights on the bottom two rows do the heavy lifting.

🗣

"Here's all five paid landing pages. Top three get the most traffic — request-info, bachelor's, military. Engagement in the 40s and 50s. Conversion around 3%. That's where most of our ad dollars land."

🎯

Point to the green numbers at the bottom.

💥

"Now look at the bottom two. Way less traffic. But 75% engagement, 7 to 13% conversion. These two pages — with a fraction of the sessions — produce nearly as many conversions as the top three combined. Let that sink in."

🗣

"A better page beats a bigger budget. If request-info-v5 converted at even 7% — not 13, just 7 — that's 47,000 more leads from traffic we're already paying for."

"Intent is part of it, but both pages get paid traffic from similar campaigns. The engagement rates tell the real story — 74% vs. 46%. Visitors on the content-rich pages stay, explore, and convert. Visitors on the generic pages bounce. The page is doing the work, not just the audience."

➡

"And it's not just paid. Let me show you what's happening with our content."

6
149K Readers. 21 Conversions.
~1.5 min
💥

"This one's my favorite. We have a blog post — 'PhD vs. Doctorate' — ranks number three on Google. 149,000 people read it last year. They stay for eight minutes. 68% engagement. And it produced… 21 leads."

🎯

Pause. Let "21" land. The room will react.

🗣

"Why? Because the page never mentions we're a university. No link to our doctoral program. No CTA. Nothing. 149,000 people spend 8 minutes reading about PhDs on our site and we just… let them leave. We forgot to tell them we sell PhDs."

🎯

Point to the 2,437 number on the right.

🗣

"Across three blog posts, 243,000 sessions, 84 conversions. If we just add one contextual CTA per post — 'Hey, we offer this degree, want to learn more?' — and get to 1%, that's 2,400 leads a year. A 29x improvement. From content that already works."

💥

"This is the easiest win on the entire list. The content ranks. It engages. We just need to add a link."

"For branded or high-intent traffic, 1% would be low. For informational, it's conservative — but the current number isn't 'low conversion,' it's 'no conversion path.' We're at 0.01%. Even 0.5% gives us 1,200 leads. Twenty-one is basically zero."

➡

"And here's why all of this is getting more urgent."

7
Organic Is Declining
~1 min
🎯

Let the red numbers land. Point across the three cards, then gesture to the homepage screenshot on the right.

🗣

"So on top of everything I just showed you, organic is declining. In six months — homepage down 42%. Blog, nearly 50%. Writing center, 32%. That's our homepage right there — the front door — losing almost half its organic visitors."

💥

"This is AI overviews. Google is now answering 'what is a PhD' and 'how to write a business plan' right in the search results. People don't need to click anymore. So the free traffic we used to count on? It's shrinking."

🗣

"Which means every visit that does make it to our site is more valuable than it was a year ago. We can't afford to waste them on pages that bounce 53%."

"Good question. The login migration is a portal change for existing students — it wouldn't affect the blog or writing center, which are prospective student pages. Those are all down the same way. The pattern points to AI overview cannibalization across informational queries."

➡

"Alright, so — we know the pages underperform, and we know organic is declining. Here's the good news: we already have proof of what works."

10
Where to Focus
~2 min
💡

Move through these briskly. The room has context now — just connect each priority to what you've already shown them. Don't re-explain the data.

💥

P1: "Number one — fix the primary RFI page. That's the million-session page bouncing at 53%. Enrich it with program content and social proof. This is the single biggest lever."

🗣

P2: "Add CTAs to the blog content. The PhD post, the business plan post. One in-content module per post, connecting readers to the actual program. That's the easiest win we have."

🗣

P3: "Mobile-first above the fold. 60% of our traffic is on phones and we're underperforming everywhere on mobile. Chat widget overlap, form placement, viewport — all need attention."

🗣

P4: "Take what works on /online-degrees and /degree-programs and apply it everywhere else. We have the playbook — we just haven't run it."

🗣

P5: "And longer-term — organic decline and PMax efficiency both need strategic review alongside the reskin."

💡

If anyone asks: P1 and P2 are quick wins before any reskin. They're also the test — if enriching the RFI page works, it validates the entire approach.
