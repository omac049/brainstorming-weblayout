4
We Tested This — The Answer Is a Hybrid
~2 min
💡

This is the "we already know" slide. Leading with the A/B test builds massive credibility — it shows we're not guessing or repeating failed experiments. The conclusion isn't "replace v5 with v7" — it's "build a hybrid." Kevin's team (Kanahoma) already recommended this.

🎯

Left side shows the A/B test results: v5 at $37 CPI vs v7 at $40 CPI. Right side shows the hybrid recommendation checklist. Let the room read both.

🗣

"Before we go further — we already tested this. Last November. Kanahoma ran an A/B test on Meta traffic. Sent the same audience to both pages."

🗣

"Result: the simple, form-forward page — request-info-v5 — actually won. $37 per inquiry versus $40. Not statistically significant, but directional. On Meta, simplicity beats content depth."

💥

"So the answer isn't 'replace v5 with v7.' The answer is a hybrid. Keep v5's conversion focus — the emotional hero, the visible form, the single CTA. But add the trust elements that help people act: a few value props, credibility callouts, a testimonial. Not more content. Better content, placed strategically."

🎯

Point to the green checklist on the right. The checkmarks are what v5 already does well. The plus signs are what gets added.

🗣

"Checkmarks — what v5 already does right. Emotional imagery, form visibility, minimal distractions. Plus signs — what we layer in. Scannable value props. Credibility. A testimonial. That's the hybrid."

"It won on CPI for Meta — but the 53% bounce rate means we're still losing over half. And that's just Meta. For Search and Display traffic, where visitors arrive with more intent, the content-rich pages do convert better. The hybrid gives us the best of both: Meta-friendly simplicity with the trust signals that help across all channels."

➡

"So let me show you the system-wide pattern — because it's not just about these two pages."
